I am a senior researcher (*directeur de recherche*) at the [Computer Science department](https://www.di.ens.fr/) of [École Normale Supérieure](https://www.ens.psl.eu/) in Paris, where I lead a [research group](./group/) working at the intersection of machine learning and statistical mechanics, with a particular interest in developing mathematical theory for modern machine learning models.

My group is based at the interdisciplinary [Centre for Data Science](https://csd.ens.psl.eu/), and is part of the [DATA team](https://www.di.ens.fr/data/). I and also a *Professeur Attaché* at Université Paris Sciences et Lettres (PSL), where I [teach](./teaching.md) in undergraduate and graduate programmes.

You can find more about my [group](./group/) and a complete list of my [publications](./publications/).
